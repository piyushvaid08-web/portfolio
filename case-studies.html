Digital Marketing

Growth Campaign Concept

The Challenge: A premium direct-to-consumer brand needed to launch in a highly competitive digital landscape, facing skyrocketing acquisition costs and poor initial organic search visibility.

The Solution: Piyush Vaid developed a comprehensive SEO framework coupled with highly targeted Meta ad campaigns. By targeting intent-focused, low-competition keywords and crafting luxurious, narrative-driven ad creatives, the acquisition strategy prioritized high-value customers.

The Outcome: Over a 90-day execution period, the brand achieved a 140% boost in organic visibility, generated a 3.2x return on ad spend (ROAS), and established a sustainable foundation for customer retention.

Branding

Luxury Identity System

The Challenge: An emerging fragrance business struggled with market perception, appearing generic and unable to charge the premium margins necessary for a boutique brand.

The Solution: Piyush Vaid reconstructed the brand's identity system. This included designing a minimalist, high-contrast visual brand asset system, formulating an elegant packaging standard, and detailing a sensory storytelling guide that repositioned the fragrance as a luxury collector's item.

The Outcome: Repositioning successfully enabled a 60% increase in product pricing. The brand gained traction in regional premium boutiques and established a highly desirable visual presence.

Website Design

Immersive AI Website

The Challenge: The client's original web presence was slow, unresponsive, and visually outdated, causing a 65% drop-off rate on landing pages.

The Solution: Piyush Vaid developed a highly responsive, custom portfolio platform. Integrating performance optimization techniques, GSAP scrolling animations, clean custom cursors, and an interactive Three.js 3D backdrop, the build combined design aesthetics with engineering speed.

The Outcome: Average load speed dropped from 4.2 seconds to 1.1 seconds. Landing page drop-off rates fell by 35% while visitor engagement time increased by 150%.

Photography

Editorial Product Story

The Challenge: A premium lifestyle brand struggled with standard, flat-lit product catalogs that failed to communicate quality and mood on their primary digital storefront.

The Solution: Piyush Vaid directed and shot a series of high-contrast editorial product photographs. By utilizing dramatic lighting, shadows, and reflections, each photo was designed to evoke a sensory experience suited for high-end digital marketing campaigns.

The Outcome: The visual suite was deployed across web and social assets, driving a 50% increase in social media engagement and elevating the brand's aesthetic perception.

Editing Direction

Videography

Cinematic Brand Film

The Challenge: Communicating the premium nature of a fragrance brand within a short, 45-second video context without resorting to generic advertising tropes.

The Solution: Piyush Vaid directed and edited a cinematic brand film. Focusing heavily on macro details—liquid motion, light playing on glass bottles, and textured surfaces—the film established an emotional connection using ambient sound design and rhythmic pacing.

The Outcome: The campaign achieved over 100,000 organic views on digital platforms, driving direct traffic to the brand's digital store and establishing a high-prestige brand image.

AI Projects

Prompt-to-Experience Systems

The Challenge: Designing marketing assets, product mockups, and content layouts manually for multiple campaigns was consuming hundreds of designer hours and slowing speed-to-market.

The Solution: Piyush Vaid designed a custom generative AI pipeline. By integrating prompt engineering with automated image synthesis models and layout grids, the system allows rapid prototyping of product and branding scenarios.

The Outcome: Mockup and campaign concept generation time was reduced by 80%, enabling immediate visual validation of new product variants and marketing themes.
